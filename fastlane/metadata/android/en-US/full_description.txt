The Pill is a keyboard coach for messages to your partner and family.

You are tired, a child is crying, and a message comes out sharper than you meant. Before you hit send, The Pill checks the tone. If the draft sounds like an attack, it shows a short, kind note and two calmer versions that keep every fact: times, names, amounts and your request.

• Speaks up only when a message sounds like an attack: sarcasm, blame, shouting, ultimatums.
• Two alternatives in the first person, ready to tap. Nothing is replaced unless you tap it.
• Never sends or rewrites anything on its own.
• Stays silent in password and other sensitive fields.
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• The language model runs on the operator's own hardware, not a third-party AI service. The server does not store or log your messages.

To turn it on: Settings → Keyboard → add The Pill, and on iPhone allow Full Access so the keyboard can reach the server. Then enter the server address in the app.

The app and the privacy policy come in 45 languages. Suggestions are written in the language of your message; they are tuned for Czech first.
